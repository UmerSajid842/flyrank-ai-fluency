## What to return here

After the reviewer responds, preserve their exact wording in a screenshot or copied message with the date visible if possible. Add that material to the evidence folder and record:

1. Who reviewed it in non-sensitive terms, such as “classmate” or “mentor”.
2. The unedited feedback.
3. Whether it is a must-fix or a nice-to-have.
4. The exact change made, or why a requested change was not applicable.

A genuine must-fix item must be fixed and retested before the FlyRank card is submitted.
