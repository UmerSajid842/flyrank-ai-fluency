# The Through-Line — Content and CTA Map

## One memorable claim

> **I build inspectable ML prototypes that turn messy content signals into a clearer prioritisation decision.**

The claim is intentionally narrower than “I improve SEO” or “I build production AI.” It is supported by the public capstone: a leakage-aware content-prioritisation prototype evaluated across seven held-out clients, where reported Precision@50 rose from 0.340 for a baseline to 0.540 for the model.[1]

## Primary action

**Open the case study.**

This is the same action used in the Week 1 proof statement. Every portfolio CTA either leads directly to the case study or gives a reader the evidence needed to decide whether to open it.

## Content ordering rules

| Rule | Practical decision |
|---|---|
| Strongest work leads | The live capstone case study is the first and initially only featured case. |
| Result before tool list | The reported Precision@50 comparison appears before listing Python, pandas, scikit-learn, or notebooks. |
| Method includes limits | The case study names the prototype boundary and avoids claims of production deployment or causal business impact. |
| One action per screen | Primary buttons say “Open the case study”; source links use secondary text treatment. |
| Links are inspectable | GitHub and live-paper links are visible rather than hidden behind an email gate. |
