# Image Curation — Umer Sajid Portfolio

## Curation principle

> **Show the actual work first.** Charts, notebooks, and the live case study are evidence. Generated visuals may provide a small connective cue, but they never stand in for the model, the data process, or the result.

## Image-needs list

| Portfolio location | Needed visual | Source decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home hero | Compact U monogram and a short evidence-first claim | **AI-generated connective visual** | The small monogram gives the portfolio a repeatable navigation cue without pretending to represent technical work. |
| Featured case-study card | Precision@50 comparison chart | **Real work capture** | It shows the public, verified comparison: baseline Precision@50 of 0.340 and model Precision@50 of 0.540.[1] |
| Case-study methodology section | Feature-importance chart | **Real work capture** | It is an output of the capstone analysis and creates a route from claim to inspectable evidence. |
| Case-study detail | Notebook / report / repository links | **Real links, not an image** | A link is more useful and more honest than a decorative screenshot when readers need to inspect the method. |
| About section | Personal portrait | **Not included yet** | A real portrait must be supplied by Umer before one is added; no generated avatar will be used. |
| Contact / CTA | No additional visual | **No image needed** | The action should stay clear: open the case study or contact Umer through a verified link. |

## One image rejected

**Rejected:** an AI-generated hero illustration of abstract dashboards, neural networks, or a person coding.

**Reason:** it would look polished but would not show Umer’s actual work, method, or result. It could make the portfolio seem less trustworthy by substituting a generic visual for the public case-study evidence. The real Precision@50 chart is more specific, legible, and defensible.

## Real capture over AI: explicit call

For the featured project card, the **real Precision@50 chart** is used instead of an AI-generated “model improvement” image. The chart is public-safe, directly connected to the public report, and can be checked against the live case study. Generated imagery is limited to the small U monogram, where it functions as a visual connector rather than a claim of work output.

## Presentation rules

- Keep chart labels and axes visible; do not crop away context to make a more aesthetic thumbnail.
- Use alt text that states what the chart shows, not an inflated conclusion. Example: “Precision@50 comparison: baseline 0.340; model 0.540 on seven held-out clients.”
- Do not apply decorative filters, fake dashboard frames, or modifications that change the charts’ meaning.
- Add a real portrait only after Umer supplies one and approves its portfolio use.
