## Real work included

The ML case study uses the actual capstone Precision@50 chart rather than an AI stand-in. The public-safe case study reports **0.340** for the baseline and **0.540** for the model across seven held-out clients. This is explicitly framed as a model-evaluation result, not a production, traffic, revenue, SEO, or causal-impact claim. The public paper and source repository remain the primary inspectable evidence.

The AI Work page presents an evidence-safety companion that gives advisory checks on public portfolio copy. Its documented evaluation covers seven cases, and its verification record keeps final review with a human rather than granting publishing authority.

## Real-person reaction and response

A real friend reviewed the live ML portfolio and the hardening log. The reviewer is not named here for privacy. Their unedited feedback was:

> I reviewed your ML portfolio and the hardening log.
>
> Overall, the site is easy to understand and the navigation is straightforward. The case studies are clearly presented, and the evidence links are useful for verifying the projects. The GitHub repository-check button also works as expected.
>
> I did not notice any major broken links or confusing navigation issues. The project descriptions give enough context to understand what you worked on without needing a lot of additional explanation.
>
> One small improvement would be to make the most important project outcomes and results slightly more prominent, so a recruiter can understand the impact of each project more quickly.
>
> Overall, I think the portfolio is in good shape and does not have any major user-facing problems.

The actionable suggestion was not treated as a claim of measured employer impact. Instead, the homepage and AI Work page now show a high-contrast **Verified result** summary directly after the introductory copy. It surfaces the documented ML evaluation improvement and the evidence-safety agent’s bounded outcome, while retaining their source and limitation language. The change was published and checked at a 390 px browser viewport; the summary remains legible and does not create horizontal overflow. The detailed related record is available in [the Week 9 structured peer-review and fix log](../CUSTOM-MQX0LDQC-0D807C09/structured_peer_review_and_fix.md).

## Still ugly — honest list

The portfolio is intentionally small and retains known gaps. A professional LinkedIn destination, reviewed CV, and verified booking link have not been supplied, so they are not fabricated. The Notes page has no posts because no source-backed posts are ready. There is one ML case study and one AI Work case rather than a broader portfolio series. The contact page provides routes rather than a server-backed contact form. The site has no measured production, traffic, revenue, SEO-ranking, or causal-impact outcome, and it makes no such claim.
